## What it detects

Detects a member being added to the built-in local Administrators group (Security 4732), often done after creating a backdoor account.

## How it works

- `selection`: `EventID` equals `4732` AND `TargetUserName` equals one of `Administrators`, `Administratoren`, `Administrateurs`
- condition: `selection`
