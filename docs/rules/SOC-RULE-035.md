## What it detects

Detects the PSEXESVC service binary (or renamed PsExec-like services) starting, the hallmark of remote execution over SMB with PsExec or its clones.

## How it works

- `selection_img`: `Image` ends with `\PSEXESVC.exe`
- `selection_parent`: `ParentImage` ends with `\PSEXESVC.exe`
- condition: `1 of selection_*`
