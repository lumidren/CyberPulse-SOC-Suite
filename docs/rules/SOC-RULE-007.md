# SOC-RULE-007: Kerberoasting TGS Ticket Request with RC4 Encryption

> Generated by `python scripts/rule_docs.py` from the rule and the test evidence.

## What it detects

Detects suspicious Kerberos Service Ticket (TGS) requests with RC4-HMAC (0x17) encryption targeting Service Principal Names (SPNs), characteristic of Kerberoasting attacks.

## How it works

- `selection`: `EventID` equals `4769` AND `TicketOptions` equals `0x40810000` AND `TicketEncryptionType` equals `0x17`
- `filter`: `ServiceName` ends with `$`
- condition: `selection and not filter`
