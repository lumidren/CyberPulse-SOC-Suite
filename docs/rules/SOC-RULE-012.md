# SOC-RULE-012: Pass-the-Hash Logon Patterns (NTLM and seclogo)

> Generated by `python scripts/rule_docs.py` from the rule and the test evidence.

**Level** high · **ATT&CK** T1550.002 · **Version** 1.0.0 · `rules/sigma/win_pass_the_hash.yml` · [Runbook](../runbooks/SOC-RULE-012.md)

## What it detects

Detects logon events typical of pass-the-hash: a LogonType 9 (NewCredentials) logon via the seclogo process with the Negotiate package, as produced by tools that inject an NTLM hash into a new logon session, or a network NTLM logon with a zero session key length.

## How it works

- `selection_seclogo`: `EventID` equals `4624` AND `LogonType` equals `9` AND `LogonProcessName` equals `seclogo` AND `AuthenticationPackageName` equals `Negotiate`
- `selection_ntlm`: `EventID` equals `4624` AND `LogonType` equals `3` AND `AuthenticationPackageName` equals `NTLM` AND `KeyLength` equals `0` AND `LogonProcessName` equals `NtLmSsp`
- `filter_anonymous`: `TargetUserName` equals `ANONYMOUS LOGON`
- condition: `1 of selection_* and not filter_anonymous`
