## What it detects

Detects creation of a WMI event filter, consumer or binding (Sysmon 19/20/21) with a command-line or script consumer, a stealthy persistence technique.

## How it works

- `selection_consumer`: `EventID` equals `20` AND `Type` equals one of `Command Line`, `Script`
- `selection_binding`: `EventID` equals `21`
- condition: `1 of selection_*`
