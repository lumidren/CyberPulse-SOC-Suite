# SOC-RULE-011: Vulnerable Signed Driver Load (BYOVD Evasion)

> Generated by `python scripts/rule_docs.py` from the rule and the test evidence.

**Level** critical · **ATT&CK** T1068 · **Version** 1.1.0 · `rules/sigma/win_driver_byovd_load.yml` · [Runbook](../runbooks/SOC-RULE-011.md)

## What it detects

Detects loading of known vulnerable signed drivers (e.g., gdrv.sys, mhyprot2.sys) commonly leveraged in Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver (BYOVD) attacks to disable endpoint security agents from kernel-mode.

## How it works

- `selection`: `ImageLoaded` ends with one of `\gdrv.sys`, `\mhyprot2.sys`, `\procexp.sys`, `\RTCore64.sys`, `\DBUtil_2_3.sys`
- `filter`: `Signed` equals `true` AND `SignatureStatus` equals `Valid` AND `ImageLoaded` starts with `C:\Windows\System32\drivers\`
- condition: `selection and not filter`

## Telemetry it needs

Sysmon Event 6 (driver load) (logsource `windows/driver_load`). If that source is not collected, the rule cannot fire: see [DATA_SOURCES.md](../coverage/DATA_SOURCES.md).
